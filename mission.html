[01] Our Mission __

Stable physics, complex software, 
simple user interfaces

Our job is to put trustworthy subsurface intelligence in an operator's hands, in the field, faster than anyone else.

Every Douglas Mining sensor is built around one well-understood piece of physics, so the result you see in the field is one you can trust.

Magnetometry
A Hall effect magnetometer outputs a voltage proportional to the magnetic field around it, and it knows which way the field points. Scan an area and anything that disturbs the field stands out as an outlier: ore bodies, buried metal, explosive devices, or the empty space of a void.

Resistivity
Push a current through the ground and measure how hard it has to work. A stone foundation resists the flow; the damp organic fill of a midden conducts it more easily than the soil around it. Take enough readings at the surface and electrical resistivity tomography turns them into a cross-section of what is underneath, drawn like a topographic map.

We build to a field standard. Compact enough to carry in, rugged enough to survive the trip, quick to set up and quick to learn. Units share parts wherever they can, so a failure in the field is fixed with a swap, not a shipment, and the team stays on mission.
