[3.1] Volunteer __

Request to Volunteer

If you have an appetite for discovery and can hold up under field conditions, we want you on a deployment.

We run archaeological surveys, cave digs, and surface and subsurface exploration, sometimes far from a road and always as a small team. If you hold a U.S. passport, email:

enlist@douglasmining.com

 and brief us on:

  • Region or specific location of interest (e.g. within Austin city limits, Guatemala, strictly Mayan temples)
  • Operational Tempo (e.g. “I like to work in short bursts and sleep in my own bed at night”, “I don’t mind sleeping in a tent so long as someone else pays for my food”)
  • Unique Skills (e.g. drone experience, digging experience, previous field work, combat experience, child raising experience)
  • Financial Situation (e.g. “I am happy to pay-my-way on a multi-day expedition” or “I am happy to do difficult work for minimum wage under the expectation of being a part of a discovery”)

[02] Capabilities By Domain __

Your mission is our mission. We put subsurface intelligence in the hands of the people who need it most: defense and security teams locating unexploded ordnance, concealed tunnels, and buried caches before anyone is standing on top of them. The same instruments and the same discipline serve industrial teams mapping what is under a site, and researchers who need survey-grade data in hard places. Every kit is built to deploy fast, run in the field, and come home.

[2.1] Military Applications

Magnetometry and resistivity surveys that find what is under the ground before your people are on top of it: unexploded ordnance, concealed tunnels, buried caches. Readings are processed on site, so the team gets an answer where it stands, not back at base. Kits are compact, ruggedized, and can be tested to your agency's own protocols before deployment.

[2.2] Industrial Applications

Instruments built for hostile ground make a job site easy ground. Magnetometry and resistivity surveys map foundations, pipelines, voids, and buried utilities before you build on them, dig through them, or rely on them. Readings become a picture your engineers can plan against, so problems are found on a screen rather than in the ground.

[2.3]  Research & Development

Survey-grade data from places that don't cooperate: caves, remote sites, terrain with no power and no road in.
