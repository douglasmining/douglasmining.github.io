The basic principles of resistivity

Push metal probes into the ground and pass a current between them. The ground resists that current, and the resistance is measured in ohms. What you find depends on how hard the current has to work: a high-resistance anomaly points to a void, cave, or tunnel; a low-resistance anomaly points to metal, minerals, or water.

Imaging Basics:

In order to create an Image with the device, 8 to 16 probes are inserted into the ground and all possible combinations are scanned. The device guides you through this process and automatically creates an image of what is underground.
