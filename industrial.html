Training Requisition

Already have a unit? Send the details below to your Douglas Mining point of contact. If you don't have a contact yet, or don't own a unit, say so in the first line of an email to:

train@douglasmining.com

When we receive it, you will get:

  • A requisition number for tracking the request.
  • The date it was received.
  • A point of contact at Douglas Mining who will plan and deliver the training.

Include what applies. Partial submissions are welcome; we will ask for anything else we need.

Requestor Information

  • Name of the person or department requesting the training.
  • Contact information (phone number, email).
  • Date of the request.
  • Title or name of the training program.

Training Schedule

  • Proposed date(s) and time(s) for the training.
  • Duration of the training (e.g., half-day, full-day, multiple sessions).
  • Flexibility in scheduling (e.g., alternative dates or times).

Training Location

  • Preferred venue for the training (e.g., on-site, off-site, online).
  • Any special requirements for the location (e.g., room setup, equipment needs).

Required Resources

  • Materials requested for the training (e.g., manuals, online resources).
  • Equipment requirements (e.g., computers, projectors, internet access).
  • Support services (e.g., IT support, catering).

Cost and Budget

  • Estimated cost of the training program.
  • Budget allocation or source of funding.
  • Approval for expenditure, if required.

Approval and Authorization

  • Who approved the request and when (e.g., department head, HR manager).
  • Anything your organization requires on the day, such as waivers, badges, or site passes.

Additional or Special Instructions

  • Post-training reporting requests (e.g., submission of certificates, training completion reports).
  • Any other relevant information that may affect the training (e.g., prerequisites, special accommodations).

Participant Details (if known)

  • List of participants, including names and roles.
  • Any specific needs or considerations for individual participants (e.g., movement restrictions, accessibility needs).

[2.1] Military Applications

Magnetometry and resistivity surveys that find what is under the ground before your people are on top of it: unexploded ordnance, concealed tunnels, buried caches. Our software separates the signal from background noise so a team can act on it in the field, not back at base.

[2.2] Industrial Applications

Magnetometry and resistivity surveys that map what is under a site before you build on it, dig through it, or rely on it: foundations, pipelines, voids, and buried utilities. Our software turns raw readings into a picture your engineers can plan against, so problems are found on a screen rather than in the ground.
